# Code of conduct

liba32android welcomes technical contributions, bug reports, research evidence,
and review from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique code, evidence, APIs, and engineering
decisions rather than people. Assume good intent, but be willing to correct
mistakes when evidence changes.

Technical disagreement is normal. Keep it productive by providing reproducible
facts, explaining tradeoffs, and separating observed behavior from inference.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted insults, doxxing, sexualized
attention, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project
spaces.

Do not pressure contributors to share proprietary binaries, private data,
credentials, or other material they are not authorized to disclose.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate
these expectations and may restrict participation for repeated or severe
violations.

For sensitive conduct concerns, use a private GitHub contact path where
available rather than posting personal details publicly.
